The routine

  1. Save your starter. Set the SCOBY aside in about two cups of kombucha for the next batch.
  2. Flavor the bottles. Use the chart above, at 10–20% of the bottle.
  3. Fill. Stir, then funnel the kombucha in, leaving about an inch of headspace.
  4. Seal and wait. Clamp the tops shut. Room temperature, out of sun, for 2–4 days.
  5. Test one. After two days, chill one bottle and open it. Fizzy enough? Fridge the rest.
  6. Refrigerate. Cold slows fermentation and settles the carbonation in.

Gusher-proof checklist

  • Thick, round bottles made for carbonation, with no chips or cracks
  • Gaskets soft and seated flat all the way around
  • A reminder set for day 2. Forgotten bottles cause most gushers
  • Fast fruits (mango, pineapple, ginger) checked early
  • Unsure? Burp each bottle briefly once a day
  • Bottles fermenting in a cabinet or cooler, not on an open shelf
  • Chilled before opening, opened over the sink, hand on the cap

Flat?

Tired gasket: the most common cause. Replace it.

Cool room: find a warmer spot or give it another day.

Too little sugar: add a bit more fruit or juice.

Too much headspace: fill to about an inch.
