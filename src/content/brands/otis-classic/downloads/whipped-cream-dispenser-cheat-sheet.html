Whipped Cream Dispenser Cheat Sheet

For both Otis Classic 500 ml (one pint) dispensers. Print it and tape it inside a cabinet door.
Fill · Charge · Shake · ServeAbout 60 seconds,
start to finish

The routine

  1. Chill. Cold cream whips better. Chill the empty dispenser too if you have a few minutes.
  2. Fill. Up to one pint of heavy cream, never past the max fill line. The gas needs room.
  3. Sweeten. 2 tbsp powdered sugar + 1 tsp vanilla. Use powdered sugar or syrup, not granulated.
  4. Seal. Check the gasket sits flat inside the head. Screw the head on firmly and evenly. Add a tip.
  5. Charge. One 8 g N₂O charger in the holder. Screw it on until you hear the hiss.
  6. Shake. Four to six firm shakes, then stop. More is not better.
  7. Dispense. Fully upside down, tip pointing at the plate. Squeeze gently. Soft? Shake once or twice more.

The three rules

N₂Oonly
Cream chargers must be N₂O (nitrous oxide). CO₂ (soda) and N₂ (nitro coffee) cartridges look alike but won't whip cream.
30–36%fat
Heavy cream or heavy whipping cream. Half-and-half, milk and low-fat creams come out thin and foamy.
≤ 50°Fcold
Straight from the fridge. One charger per pint, even if you only fill it halfway.

Fix it

ProblemLikely cause → fix
RunnyCream not cold or not rich enough → use cold heavy cream. Or it needs 1–2 more shakes.
Stiff or sputteringOver-shaken → next time stop at four shakes and test.
Gas escaping at the headGasket missing or not flat → release pressure, reseat it.
Nothing comes outClogged tip (sugar or spice) → release pressure, brush the tip and valve.

Clean & store

Release all pressure before opening. Upright over the sink, squeeze until there's no cream and no hiss. Then unscrew the head.

Remove the tip, tip base and gasket. Wash in warm soapy water, brush out the tips and valve, and dry fully.

Storing? A charged dispenser keeps in the fridge for up to about a week, within the cream's use-by date. Shake once or twice before use.

Safety: cream chargers are for food preparation only. Never open a dispenser that's under pressure, never use more chargers than it calls for, and keep chargers away from heat. Strain anything with seeds, zest or sugar before filling.
