Here's your [Second Fermentation Flavor Chart]({{lead_magnet_url}}). It has starting amounts for eight flavors, a gusher-proof checklist and a batch log for your notes. Print it and keep it by wherever you brew.

The basics, for a 16 oz bottle:

1. **Flavor at 10–20%.** That's 2–3 tablespoons of fruit purée or chopped fruit, or up to a quarter cup of juice.
2. **Leave about an inch of headspace.** Too much means less fizz, and too little leaves no room for pressure.
3. **Two to four days at room temperature,** out of direct sun. Then chill it for several hours before you open it.

The full walkthrough is in our [kombucha second fermentation guide](/blog/kombucha-second-fermentation-guide/).
