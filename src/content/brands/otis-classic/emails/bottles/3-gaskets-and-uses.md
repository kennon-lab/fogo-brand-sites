The rubber gasket is what makes a swing top airtight, so a little care goes a long way.

- **Check before every batch.** A gasket should be soft and sit flat all the way around. If it's hard, cracked or flattened, replace it.
- **Wash gaskets by hand.** Pop them off and wash them in warm soapy water, then let them dry before you refit them.
- **Store bottles with the top open.** Leaving them clamped shut in the cupboard keeps the gasket squashed and wears it out faster.

### Five things to bottle between batches

1. **Water kefir, ginger beer and homemade sodas.** Anything that carbonates in the bottle uses the same method as kombucha.
2. **Cold brew concentrate.** It stays fresh and pours cleanly.
3. **Simple syrups:** vanilla, ginger, rosemary, or whatever your cocktails call for.
4. **Homemade vanilla extract.** Split vanilla beans, vodka or bourbon, and patience.
5. **Limoncello.** It also makes a very good gift in a labeled bottle.
