All of our swing-top bottles are 16 oz and come in sets of six. They're thick, round glass with a gasket seal. The only choices are the glass and the cap.

**Clear or amber glass?**

- **[Clear](amazon:B01LB1862A)** lets you watch what's happening: fruit, sediment, fill level, fizz. It's the best choice while you're learning second fermentation.
- **[Amber](amazon:B01DM1J6I0)** tints out light. Choose it for light-sensitive flavors, for storing extracts, or because you like the look on a shelf.

**Plastic or ceramic caps?**

- **Plastic caps** are the lightweight everyday workhorse. The clear plastic-cap set also includes a marker, labels and six spare gaskets.
- **[Ceramic caps](amazon:B076PKTRXV)** are porcelain. They're heavier, look classic, and hold up to years of clamping.

**How many do you need?** Six 16 oz bottles hold most of a one-gallon batch. If you brew a gallon at a time, two sets let one batch ferment while the other chills.
