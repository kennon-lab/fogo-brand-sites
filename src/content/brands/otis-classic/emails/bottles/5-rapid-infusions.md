Your swing tops are perfect for storing homemade infusions: chili tequila, basil gin, orange-peel bourbon. Normally those take days to steep. With a whipped cream dispenser, they take about two minutes.

Here's how it works. Put a cup of spirit and your aromatics in the dispenser and charge it. The pressure pushes liquid into the aromatics. When you release it, the liquid rushes back out and carries the flavor with it. Swirl for about a minute, rest it for a minute or two, then hold it upright with a cup over the nozzle and release the gas slowly. Strain it into a clean bottle, label it, and you're done.

A dispenser does a lot more than cocktails: cold foam for coffee, chocolate mousse in minutes, and flavored whipped creams.
