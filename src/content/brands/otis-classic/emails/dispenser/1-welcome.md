Here's your [Whipped Cream Dispenser Cheat Sheet]({{lead_magnet_url}}). It fits on one page and it's made to be printed and taped inside a cabinet door.

If you only remember three things, make them these:

1. **Cold heavy cream, 30–36% fat.** Half-and-half and milk don't have enough fat to hold the gas.
2. **One 8 g N₂O charger per pint.** Not CO₂, not N₂, and not two.
3. **Four to six shakes, then stop.** Over-shaking is the most common cause of stiff, grainy cream.
