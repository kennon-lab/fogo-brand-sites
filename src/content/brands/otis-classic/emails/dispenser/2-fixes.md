If your first batch didn't come out quite right, you're in good company. Almost every dispenser problem is one of these four:

**Runny cream.** The cream wasn't cold enough or high-fat enough, or it needs one or two more shakes. Use heavy cream straight from the fridge.

**Too stiff, or sputtering.** It's been over-shaken. Next time, stop at four shakes and test before adding more.

**Gas escaping around the head.** The gasket is missing or isn't sitting flat inside the head. Release the pressure, reseat it, and try again.

**Nothing comes out.** The tip is probably clogged, usually by undissolved sugar or spice. Release the pressure, then clear the tip and valve with the cleaning brush. Powdered sugar and syrups dissolve fully; granulated sugar often doesn't.

### The one rule that matters more than the rest

**Release all the pressure before you open it.** Hold the dispenser upright over the sink and squeeze the lever until there's no cream and no hiss. Only then unscrew the head.

To clean, take off the tip, the tip base and the gasket, wash everything in warm soapy water, and brush out the tips and valve. Let the parts dry completely before you put it back together.
