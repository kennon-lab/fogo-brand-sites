A whipped cream dispenser dissolves gas into a liquid under pressure, then releases it as a foam. Whipped cream is just the most famous result. Here are three more to try this week.

**Coffee-shop cold foam.** Half a cup of heavy cream, a quarter cup of milk and 1–2 tablespoons of vanilla syrup. Charge it, shake only two or three times so it stays pourable, and float it over cold brew or an iced latte.

**Chocolate mousse in minutes.** Gently warm one cup of heavy cream and stir in about 3 ounces of chopped dark chocolate until smooth. Sweeten to taste, then chill it completely, for a few hours or overnight. Strain, charge, shake, and dispense straight into glasses.

**Rapid cocktail infusions.** Put a cup of spirit and your aromatics in the dispenser: jalapeño in tequila, basil in gin, orange peel in bourbon. Charge it, swirl for about a minute, and rest it for another minute or two. Then hold it upright with a cup over the nozzle and release the gas slowly. Strain and taste. What usually takes days takes minutes.

One rule covers all three: **everything that goes in has to be completely smooth.** Strain through a fine-mesh sieve before you fill. Seeds, zest and undissolved sugar are what clog a nozzle.
