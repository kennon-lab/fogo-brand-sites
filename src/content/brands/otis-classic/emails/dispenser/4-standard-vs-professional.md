We make two whipped cream dispensers, and we get asked the difference a lot. Here's the short version.

**What they share.** Both hold 500 ml (one pint), both are stainless steel, and both use the same 8 g N₂O chargers. Everything in your cheat sheet works the same on either one.

**The [Standard dispenser](amazon:B01DZ2HZ2U)** comes with three decorating tips, a cleaning brush and a step-by-step guide. It's the easy pick for desserts, coffee and weekend baking.

**The [Professional dispenser](amazon:B06WVD2K6N)** is the heavier-duty build. It has reinforced high-pressure seals, a precision-flow trigger that dispenses reliably even at a slight angle, and dishwasher-safe parts. Reach for it if you use a dispenser every day, cook for a crowd, or do a lot of infusions and savory foams.

If you already have one, you don't need the other. The Professional does make a good gift for the friend who keeps borrowing yours.
