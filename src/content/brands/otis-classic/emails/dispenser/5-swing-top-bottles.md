If you like making things from scratch, there's a second tool we think you'll use just as much: a good swing-top bottle.

Thick, round glass and a rubber gasket make an airtight seal. That's what you want for flavored syrups, cold brew concentrate, homemade vanilla extract, and your dispenser infusions once they're strained. Round swing tops are also built to hold carbonation, which is why home brewers use them for kombucha.

If you're curious about brewing, our [guide to kombucha second fermentation](/blog/kombucha-second-fermentation-guide/) walks through bottling for flavor and fizz. We made a free printable to go with it: the [Second Fermentation Flavor Chart]({{lead_magnet_url:bottles}}).

Our [16 oz swing-top bottles](amazon:B01LB1862A) come in sets of six, in clear or amber glass, with plastic or ceramic caps.
